Digital Eye Strain

Visual Fatigue & Dryness

When staring at screens, your blink rate drops by up to 60% (from ~20 blinks per minute down to 7). This starves your eyes of moisture, leading to redness, headaches, blurry vision, and dry eyes.

Science Fact
Blink frequency drops from 20 to ~7 times/min.
Common Symptom
"Sand in eye" gritty feeling & tension headaches.
The Instant Reset Fix

Practice the 20-20-20 Rule: Every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.
